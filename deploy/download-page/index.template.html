Our approach

Your purchases. Your data. Your control.

We keep only what you put in the app, say plainly where it lives, and make it easy to take it with you.

Where it lives

Purchases and receipts are kept on our own server in Indonesia, run by the developer, and reach you over HTTPS through Cloudflare. That is what lets reminders arrive by e-mail and family buckets stay in sync.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting tools, no selling your data. The claim PDF is made on your phone, not on our server.

Leave whenever

Export your purchases as CSV from Settings, or delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with e-mail or Google. Your purchases are kept with your account, so they stay safe when you change phones and can be shared with family.

Is ReturnIt free?

Yes. ReturnIt is free to download and use, and it has no ads.

Does ReturnIt work offline?

ReturnIt needs an internet connection. Your purchases are kept on our server, so they survive a lost or replaced phone, reminders can reach you by e-mail, and family buckets stay in sync.

Where is my purchase data stored?

On our own server in Indonesia, run by the developer. Data travels over HTTPS and passes through Cloudflare. Receipts are scanned for malware and stored privately, and the claim package PDF is made on your phone.

The privacy policy lists everything we store and for how long.

Can I export my data?

Yes. In the app, open Settings and choose Export CSV under your account to download your purchases. You can delete your account there too.

How do I install ReturnIt?

Download the APK, open it and allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser if Android asks. See Install in three steps.

Google Play Protect shows a warning. Is it safe?

The warning appears because the app is not on Google Play yet. If you downloaded it from this page, choose “Install anyway”. You can check the file first with its SHA-256 fingerprint.

How do I update ReturnIt?

Download the new file from this page and install it over the old one; your data stays. When an update is required, the app tells you and links here.

Does ReturnIt support older Android devices?

It needs Android 7.0 or newer with a 64-bit (arm64) processor, which covers almost every phone made since 2018. Very old 32-bit phones and x86 emulators are not supported.

How do I make a warranty claim package?

Open a purchase and tap “Make a claim package”. Choose whether to include the price, the notes and the receipt photos, then share the PDF with the shop or service centre.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. For now ReturnIt is available for Android only.
